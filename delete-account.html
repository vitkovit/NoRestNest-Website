Delete your account

Applies to the NoRestNest app on Android and iOS

1. Delete from inside the app

  1. Open NoRestNest and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile → Account actions → Delete Account.
  3. Confirm. You will be asked to re-authenticate for security.

A 30-day grace period starts and other devices are signed out. During this window you can cancel the deletion from the recovery page shown at sign-in. You can also choose Delete now to skip the grace period and delete immediately.

On Android, your Google Play subscription is cancelled automatically — it will not renew. On iOS, the app opens Settings so you can cancel the subscription yourself.

2. Can't access the app?

Email privacy@norestnest.com from the address your account is registered with and request deletion. We respond within one month.

3. What is deleted, what is kept

DataOutcome
Account — email, display name, user IDDeleted after the 30-day grace period, or immediately with Delete now
All synced fitness data — workouts, programs, body measurements, food, water and meal-prep logsDeleted
Progress photos and videos in cloud storageDeleted
Subscription customer record (RevenueCat)Deleted
Referral codes listing you as inviterDeleted
Deletion audit row — request time, email, outcomeKept as proof the deletion happened
Anonymous, aggregated analytics — no user ID, no emailRetained by Firebase Analytics up to 14 months
Tax and accounting records for purchasesKept as required by EU accounting law, typically 7–10 years
Local data on your deviceNot touched — uninstall the app to remove it

4. Deleting data without deleting the account

Individual entries — workouts, measurements, photos, food and water logs — can be deleted at any time inside the app. Deletions also remove the cloud copy. Full details in the Privacy Policy.
