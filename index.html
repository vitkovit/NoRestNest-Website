07 · What it isn’t

Things NoRestNest
won’t do.

It won’t pretend to be your coach.

The math suggests the next weight. That’s it. No motivational quotes, no “you got this!” notification at 6am.

It won’t gamify your gains.

No XP, no levels, no badges. You’re not collecting points. You’re collecting muscle.

It won’t sell your data. To anyone.

No ad SDKs, no data broker, no “anonymized aggregate”. Your protein intake is not a product.
